# Acme refund policy

- Monthly plans: full refund within 30 days of the charge. After that, no refund; the plan runs to the end of the month.
- Annual plans: full refund within 30 days. After that, a prorated refund for whole unused months, minus a 10% fee.
- Chargebacks: do not promise anything; hand the case to billing.

Always state which rule applies and the amount, then the date the money lands (5-7 business days).
