# Release notes

1. Group each change under Added, Changed or Fixed. Drop chores (CI, formatting, dependency bumps) unless they change behaviour.
2. One line per change, written for a user, not a developer: say what they can now do or what stopped breaking.
3. Follow the house style in `references/style.md` for the heading, dates and links.
